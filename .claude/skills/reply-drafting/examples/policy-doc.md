# Customer Policy Document

## Introduction
This document defines the company's current rules on pricing, refunds,
and other sensitive customer-facing topics. Support agents (and the
reply-drafting skill) must treat this as the single source of truth —
never state a figure or exception that isn't written here.

This document defines **eligibility criteria and available options**.
It does not authorize the assistant to confirm outcomes for individual
customers — see Customer Communication Rules below.

## Customer Communication Rules
- The policy defines **what may be available** — it does not by
  itself establish that a particular customer qualifies.
  Customer-specific eligibility must be verified before being
  presented as confirmed.
- The AI assistant must **not** independently confirm an individual
  customer's eligibility, or approve a refund, discount, credit,
  exception, or other financial outcome. Only a human on the
  appropriate team can finalize these.
- Customer-facing replies should explain the applicable policy in
  concise, plain language, and indicate that final confirmation may
  require verification of the customer's account or purchase details.
- Processing timelines (e.g. "5–7 business days") are **internal
  estimates** and must not be presented as guaranteed customer
  outcomes. Frame them as "typically" or "usually," not as a promise.
- Where the policy does not clearly address a customer's situation,
  do not tell the customer "there is no policy" or otherwise expose
  internal policy limitations. Respond empathetically and explain
  that the situation needs to be reviewed before confirming the
  available options.

## Key Policies

### Refunds
- Our current policy provides a full refund for purchases cancelled
  within **14 days** of the initial purchase. Once the customer's
  purchase details are verified, the team can confirm the applicable
  refund.
- After 14 days and up to 30 days, a **50% refund may be discussed as
  a possible option**, subject to review and approval — this must
  never be communicated as a guaranteed outcome.
- No refunds after 30 days, except for confirmed billing errors on our end.

### Duplicate / Accidental Charges
- Our current policy provides a full refund for duplicate charges on
  the same billing cycle caused by a confirmed system error,
  regardless of the 14/30-day window. Once verified, the team can
  confirm the refund.
- Verified refunds are typically processed within **5–7 business
  days** to the original payment method — this is an internal
  estimate, not a guaranteed timeline to quote to the customer.

### Pricing
- Current plans: **Basic ($9/month)**, **Pro ($29/month)**, **Team ($79/month)**.
- Annual billing gives a **15% discount** versus monthly billing.
- We do not offer custom discounts outside of published promotions.

## Common Scenarios
- "I was charged twice" → Explain that our policy provides a full
  refund for confirmed duplicate charges; note that the team will
  confirm this once the charge is verified.
- "I want a refund after using the product for a month" → Outside the
  30-day window; explain that refunds after 30 days are limited to
  confirmed billing errors, and that this would need review.
- "Can I get a discount?" → Point to annual billing (15% off) as the
  only published discount; do not offer or imply anything beyond that.

## Exceptions
- Enterprise/Team customers with a signed contract may have custom
  terms — always check the contract before applying standard policy.
- Support leads can approve one-off exceptions, but the reply must
  flag this as "pending approval," not state it as settled.
